wn.constants.PARTS_OF_SPEECH¶

  • n – Noun

  • v – Verb

  • a – Adjective

  • r – Adverb

  • s – Adjective Satellite

  • t – Phrase

  • c – Conjunction

  • p – Adposition

  • x – Other

  • u – Unknown
